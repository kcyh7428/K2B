# Video extraction

Read [the workflow](references/workflow.md) before dispatch or processing.
This is the single maintained replacement procedure. It is prepared, not yet
accepted or activated. Until its delivery record reports relevant live acceptance,
use the preserved installed Facebook v1 procedure for Facebook; the old YouTube
text helper supplies text only and does not establish visual review.

Keith approved on-demand Mini capture, selected local browser-session use and a
tested playback-recording fallback on 8 November 2026. Facebook source duration
is at most 1,200 seconds, with a separate cumulative 256 MiB run-file cap.
YouTube duration is at most 3,600 seconds, with a cumulative 2 GiB cap. A source
can fit the duration limit and fail the byte limit. Never claim a twenty-minute
Facebook guarantee or truncate to fit.

Home and SJM independently process through their own capture-share connections.
Browser login and source playback belong to the Mini. No Home processing relay,
credential transfer, automatic retry, paid fallback or scheduled capture is enabled.
Saving an ordinary knowledge note is a separate `k2b-vault-writer` action.
